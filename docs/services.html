Our Core Services

We focus on quality framing, strong structure, and clean finishes for every project.

Deck Building & Outdoor Spaces

We design and build wood and composite decks that match your home and yard. From simple backyard spaces to multi-level decks with stairs and railings, we focus on solid framing, safe structure, and a clean, finished look.

  • Pressure-treated, cedar, or composite decking
  • Stairs, landings, and guardrails
  • Framing, joists, and blocking done to code
  • Options for privacy screens and pergolas

Garage Construction

We build attached and detached garages that are practical, durable, and ready for Calgary weather. Whether you want storage, parking, or a small workshop, we frame and finish it properly.

  • Single, double, or custom-size garages
  • Slab-on-grade or existing pad builds
  • Framing, sheathing, and roof installation
  • Insulation and interior finishing options

Basement Development

Turn an unfinished basement into a clean, usable living area. We handle framing, drywall, doors, trims, and work closely with other trades to keep your project on schedule.

  • Layout and framing for rooms and storage
  • Stair upgrades and railings
  • Drywall, taping, and basic finishing
  • Coordination with electrical and plumbing trades

Home Renovations

From small updates to larger renovations, we help you improve how your home looks and works. We focus on solid carpentry and clean finishes that blend with the rest of the house.

  • Interior framing and wall changes
  • Door, casing, and baseboard installation
  • Subfloor and sheathing installation
  • Support for kitchen and living space upgrades

Windows & Doors

Properly installed windows and doors improve comfort, security, and energy efficiency. We remove old units carefully and install new ones level, square, and well sealed.

  • New window and door installation
  • Replacement of older units
  • Trim, casing, and exterior finishing
  • Foam, flashing, and sealing against drafts

Custom Carpentry & Framing

If your project involves structural framing or custom carpentry, we can help. We pay close attention to measurements, level, and layout so everything fits correctly.

  • Wall framing and structural openings
  • Sheathing for floors, walls, and roofs
  • Prefabricated stair installation
  • Small custom projects on request

How We Work

We keep the process simple and clear so you always know what is happening on your project.

  1. We start with a conversation about your project, budget, and timeline.
  2. We visit the site, take measurements, and confirm any structural details.
  3. We provide a clear written estimate with scope of work and schedule.
  4. We complete the work with attention to safety, site cleanliness, and communication.

What You Can Expect

  • Respectful, professional crew on site
  • Work completed to building code standards
  • Clean-up of the work area at the end of the job
  • Honest updates if anything unexpected comes up
